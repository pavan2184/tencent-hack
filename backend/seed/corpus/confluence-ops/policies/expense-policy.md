Example Harbor Finance reimburses reasonable business expenses that are approved in advance where
required and claimed with an itemised receipt. This policy applies to every employee; contractors
follow the expense terms in their statement of work instead.

## Limits

- **Meals:** up to SGD 60 per person per meal when travelling or hosting a client. Alcohol is not
  reimbursable unless a director pre-approves a client event.
- **Ground transport:** taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable between 22:00 and 07:00 SGT, or
  when carrying equipment. Otherwise use public transport.
- **Flights:** economy class for trips under 6 hours; premium economy above 6 hours.
- **Hotels:** up to SGD 280 per night in Singapore and SGD 350 per night elsewhere in Asia.
- **Pre-approval:** any single trip expected to cost more than SGD 1,500 needs written approval from
  your manager before booking.

## Claims

1. Submit claims in the expense tool within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than
   90 days are rejected automatically.
2. Attach an itemised receipt for every line above SGD 25. A card slip alone is not a receipt.
3. Your manager approves the claim; Finance Ops audits a random 10% sample each month.
4. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to managers and to on-call engineers who may need to buy emergency
cloud capacity. Card spend follows the same limits and the same 30-day receipt rule. Personal use
of a corporate card, even if repaid, is a policy breach and is reported to Compliance.

Questions go to the Finance Ops team in the expenses channel.
