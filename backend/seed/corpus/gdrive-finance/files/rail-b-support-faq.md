Answers for the support desk during the Rail-B cutover window, Saturday 17 October 2026, 02:00 to
04:00 SGT. Use macro **PAY-CUTOVER-01** for replies.

## Questions and answers

- **Will my payment be delayed?** Payments submitted inside the window may take up to 15 minutes
  longer than usual. Payments outside the window are not affected.
- **Do I need to do anything?** No. Account details, limits and beneficiaries do not change.
- **Will my beneficiary see a different reference?** No. The payment reference is unchanged.
- **My payment has been pending for more than 30 minutes.** Do not send it again. Escalate the
  ticket to Payments Operations with the payment reference.
- **Is my money safe?** Yes. Customer money stays in the safeguarding account throughout.
