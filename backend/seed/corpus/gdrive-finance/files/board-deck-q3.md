Board pack for the quarter ended 30 September 2026, presented to the board on 8 October 2026.
Confidential: leadership only.

## Headline results

- **Q3 2026 revenue: SGD 48.6 million**, up **18% year on year** (Q3 2025: SGD 41.2 million).
- Revenue by line: payments SGD 29.1 million, foreign exchange SGD 12.7 million, cards
  SGD 6.8 million.
- Operating expenses: SGD 38.8 million. **EBITDA: SGD 9.8 million**, a 20.2% margin.
- Total payment volume: **SGD 12.4 billion** across 3.1 million transactions.
- Active business accounts: 41,300, up 9% on the previous quarter.

## Operations

- Rail-A reliability was the main operational risk: two SEV-2 incidents in Q3, both handled with
  the failover procedure; no customer funds were lost.
- The **Rail-B programme** has an approved budget of **SGD 1.2 million**. After the 17 October
  cutover, the target is to reduce Rail-A's share of outbound volume from 100% to 60% by Q1 2027.
- KYC refresh backlog: 640 high-risk accounts overdue at quarter end, down from 910 in Q2.

## Outlook and asks

- Q4 2026 revenue guidance: SGD 50 to 52 million.
- The board is asked to approve the FY2027 hiring plan of 24 roles, 14 of them in engineering and
  6 in Finance Ops and Compliance.
- Next board meeting: 14 January 2027.
