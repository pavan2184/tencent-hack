## Summary

Changing the `rail_router` flag takes about **4 minutes** to reach every payment worker. The
failover runbook assumes about 2 minutes. Seen in the 14 September SEV-2 (review action AI-3) and
again in the September drill.

## Root cause

Payment workers cache feature flags for **240 seconds**. The flag service pushes invalidations to
the API tier only, so workers keep routing on the old value until their cache expires.

## Fix

1. Cut the cache lifetime for routing flags from 240 seconds to **15 seconds**.
2. Subscribe payment workers to the flag-invalidation channel so a change applies at once.
3. Publish `rail_router_flag_age_seconds` per worker and alert when any worker is more than
   30 seconds behind.

## Acceptance criteria

- In staging, a `rail_router` change reaches 100% of payment workers within **30 seconds**,
  measured during the cutover rehearsal on Thursday 15 October.
- Deployed to production before the go/no-go call on Friday 16 October. If it is not, the cutover
  plan adds a manual payment-worker restart after every `rail_router` change.
